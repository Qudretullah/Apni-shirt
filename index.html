ABOUT APNI SHIRT

Your Style. Your Shirt.

Apni Shirt is a frontend learning project featuring a sample collection of casual, formal and linen shirts. Explore products, choose your options and try the demo shopping experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I select a shirt size?

Open a product page, select an available size and colour, then add the shirt to your cart.

Can I change the quantity in my cart?

Yes. Use the plus and minus buttons on the cart page to update the quantity.

Does this website accept real payments?

No. This is a frontend demo. Checkout does not process payments. Do not enter real card details.
